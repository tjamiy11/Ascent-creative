## The brief

A 60-second commercial for a third-wave roastery in Lisbon, opening five new
locations across Iberia. The brand wanted nothing literal — no beans falling
into a hopper, no espresso splash slo-mo. Just the morning, honestly observed.

<Pull>
The shot list was one line long: hands, steam, and a single pour.
</Pull>

## Approach

We embedded with the opening shift for a week before the shoot. The film is a
composite of several mornings at the same bar, edited to feel like one. No
talent, no script — only the people who actually work there.
