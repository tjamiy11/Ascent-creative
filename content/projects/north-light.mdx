## The brief

An editorial campaign for a Reykjavík furniture atelier known for restrained,
ceremonial pieces. The work needed to live inside their seasonal print catalog
and as a campaign across web and social — without ever feeling like a catalog.

<Pull>
The shortest day of the year gives you four hours of usable light. We treated
that scarcity as the art direction.
</Pull>

## Approach

Three days on location in late January. We worked exclusively with available
light — a long, low Nordic sun that wraps into rooms before retreating. Each
setup was rehearsed twice in the morning so we could shoot without
adjustments while the light held.
