## The brief

A heritage botanicals house wanted a brand film that felt closer to a short
story than a commercial. Two minutes, no voiceover, no product hero shot. We
proposed an entirely nocturnal palette and a single recurring motif: a flower
opening in the dark.

<Pull>
We wanted the audience to feel as though they had stumbled into the greenhouse at
2 a.m. — and decided not to leave.
</Pull>

## Approach

Three nights of shooting in a working glasshouse outside Antwerp. Lit with a
single 1.2k HMI bounced off muslin, plus a handful of practicals dressed into
frame. Shot on 35mm with a slightly warmed K-rate to keep the foliage feeling
alive.
